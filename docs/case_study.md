# Case Study: Historical Records to a Reviewable Event Feed

## Starting problem

Historical public facts arrive in different shapes. Economic indicators,
public awards, and vulnerability records use different identifiers, dates,
value types, and status fields. A folder of exports does not give a buyer a
consistent way to inspect quality or understand what can be relied upon.

## Public exhibit

The portfolio selects 70 unique records from three official source families,
keeps every effective date at or before December 31, 2021, and presents the
result as a consistent event-oriented exhibit.

The public materials show:

- A common view of dates, entities, event types, values, and evidence links.
- Source attribution and a clearly bounded historical publication window.
- Stable exhibit identifiers and portfolio-only record digests.
- Source coverage, time distribution, completeness, and repeated-capture
  metrics.
- An interactive report plus machine-readable CSV and JSON outputs.

## Platform extension

The companion case study shows how the same evidence discipline can support a
professional service platform: public proof for evaluation, identity and
product scope for access, controlled delivery through dashboard/API/MCP
surfaces, and term-aware customer access.

The portfolio does not claim current coverage, expose the production source, or
publish proprietary ranking, correlation, or product-generation logic.
