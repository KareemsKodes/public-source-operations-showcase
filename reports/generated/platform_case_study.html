Lifecycle

Cancellation is not premature revocation.

The access model distinguishes a cancellation request from the end of a paid period and applies the final decision everywhere.

Payment confirmedThe purchased scope becomes eligible.
Cancellation requestedService continues through the paid term.
Paid term activeAuthorized interfaces continue to work.
